What We Offer

كل اللي هتحتاجه تحت سقف واحد

Everything you need under one roof

CrossFit

Full functional training zone with turf, plyo boxes, battle ropes, rings, and climbing gear. Group and solo sessions.

Boxing

Heavy bags, speed bags, Venum gloves and pads. Whether you're sparring or burning calories, we've got you covered.

Strength

Full dumbbell rack from 5kg to 50kg+, Olympic barbells, bumper plates, squat racks, and flat benches.

Cardio

Assault air bikes, fan bikes, and conditioning circuits. High-intensity cardio without the boring treadmill.

Kettlebells

Full range from 8kg to 48kg in competition and cast iron. Swings, cleans, snatches, whatever you need.

Open Late

Doors open until 1AM. Train on your schedule, not ours. No crowds, no waiting, just you and the iron.

About the Gym

Built for people who take training seriously

Hamayel isn't a flashy commercial gym. It's a properly equipped training space in the heart of New Damietta, built by lifters for lifters. The vibe is focused: neon-lit, music loud, equipment heavy.

Whether you're into CrossFit WODs, boxing rounds, or just hitting a solid strength session, you'll find the tools and the atmosphere to push yourself.
